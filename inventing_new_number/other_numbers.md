# Other numbers

People have attacked pieces of this. You want to stand on them, not repeat them.

- **Heisenberg (1925)** literally replaced numbers with grids of numbers (matrices), where position × momentum ≠ momentum × position. The uncertainty then lives inside the multiplication rule itself. That's very close to what you want.
- **Alain Connes** built geometry from numbers where ab ≠ ba. Space itself becomes "quantum".
- **David Hestenes** (geometric algebra) turned i from a mysterious √−1 into a concrete object, a rotation in a plane. Spin falls out naturally.
- **Bob Coecke** turned quantum processes into pictures you can redraw by rules. It's a genuinely new visual language, and people learn quantum with it, schoolkids included.

None of them finished the job, and nobody has made quantum feel natural in the way you mean. There's room.
